3D Dino: the Chrome Dinosaur Game in 3D

3D Dino is a free browser game that turns the classic Chrome dinosaur (T-Rex) game into a third-person 3D platformer. Run down an endless track, jump over gaps, dodge cacti and pterodactyls, and beat your high score. No download or sign-up needed.

How to play the 3D dinosaur game

  • Press Space or the Up arrow to jump. Hold it to jump higher.
  • Press the Down arrow to duck under pterodactyls.
  • Use the Left and Right arrows (or A and D) to move sideways.
  • On a phone, use the on-screen buttons or tap to jump.

Features

Pixel-art voxel graphics, a day and night cycle every 700 points, speed that keeps rising, gaps in the track, and a high score saved in your browser.
